Input files of the bundled data set whiting_example
===================================================

North Sea whiting (Merlangius merlangus), North Sea International Bottom Trawl
Survey (NS-IBTS), quarter 1, 1983 to 2026. data-raw/whiting_example.R builds
data/whiting_example.rda from these files.

How the files were built (North Sea test of ALSCL, November 2026)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Download: DATRAS web services getCPUELength (catch per unit effort per length
per haul per hour) and getCAdata (individual length, weight, maturity and age
records), survey NS-IBTS, quarter 1, 1983 to 2026, on 12 November 2026.

data.CatL.csv
  Survey index by length bin (rows) and year (columns), in number per hour of
  trawling. Hauls in IBTS roundfish areas 1 to 7 made with the GOV trawl; hauls
  recorded with another gear were dropped (59 in 1983, 4 in 1984, 8 in 1985, 15
  in 2026). Catch per unit effort by 1-cm length class for each haul (lengths
  rounded down to the cm), with zero for hauls without whiting, was averaged
  within each ICES statistical rectangle and then across rectangles. Bin
  width: the smallest of 1, 2 and 5 cm for which the central 98% of the index
  (11 to 36 cm) spans at most 24 bins, which gives 2 cm. The end bins were
  merged inwards until fish of those lengths were caught in at least 5 hauls in
  every year: the bin labelled 9 holds all fish below 10 cm and the bin
  labelled 43 all fish of 42 cm and longer. The bin labelled 11 holds 10 cm to
  below 12 cm.

data.wgt.csv, data.mat.csv
  Weight (kg) and proportion mature at the bin labels, the same in every year,
  from the individual records of the survey: weight (g) = 0.00440 * length
  (cm)^3.183, fitted on the log scale with the correction for the mean of a
  lognormal error (85896 records, 1997 to 2026), and a logistic regression of
  maturity on length with 50% mature at 19.4 cm and 95% at 27.7 cm (145828
  records, 1983 to 2026).

survey_coverage.csv
  By year: hauls used, ICES statistical rectangles with hauls, hauls with
  whiting, and hauls_missing, the number of valid hauls in the DATRAS haul
  records (all areas and gears) that the product of catch per unit effort by
  length does not contain. 2009 lacks 63 of 398 valid hauls (63 of the 72
  German hauls) and 2012 lacks 56 of 389 (all Scottish hauls); a second
  download gave the same result. Seven other years lack 1 or 2 hauls.

age_readings_length_at_age.csv, age_readings_index_at_age.csv
  From the 205030 age readings of 1983 to 2026. Otoliths are sampled by length
  class and are not a random sample of the catch. For each year, the
  proportion at age among the fish aged in each 1-cm class (age-length key)
  was multiplied by the survey index of that class. Summed over the classes,
  this gives the index at age (second file, ages 1 to 10; older ages hold less
  than 1% of the index in every year and are left out). The mean and standard
  deviation of length at age were taken over the class midpoints for each year
  and age with at least 5 aged fish, leaving out fish recorded as a plus
  group. The first file gives the unweighted mean of these annual values over
  the years of each period (the complete series and the decades), the number
  of years and the number of aged fish. Age 1 is the first quarter 1 after the
  year of hatching. These tables do not enter a fit.

ices_M_at_age.csv, ices_maturity_at_age.csv
  Natural mortality rate (per year) and proportion mature by age and year from
  Tables 22.15 and 22.14 of the whiting section of WGNSSK 2026, as transcribed
  by the test team: ages 1 to 5 and a plus group of ages 6 and older. The
  natural mortality table ends in 2022; the maturity table covers 1991 to
  2021.

ices_assessment.csv
  Summary table of the assessment of whiting in Subarea 4 and Division 7.d
  (stock whg.27.47d, assessment of 2026, assessment key 22488) from the ICES
  Stock Assessment Database, downloaded 13 November 2026: spawning stock
  biomass (t), recruitment at age 0, mean fishing mortality rate at ages 2 to
  5 (per year) and catch (t), 1982 to 2026.

Settings recorded in the data set
---------------------------------
M = 0.50 per year from M = 4.899 * tmax^-0.916 (Then et al. 2015) with tmax =
12 years, the oldest age read at least 3 times. Number of age classes =
ceiling(log(100) / M), limited to 4 to 12, which gives 10. Survey catchability:
logistic with 50% at 10 cm and 95% at 14 cm, an assumption. cv_len fixed at
0.15 (mean coefficient of variation of length at ages 1 to 3 in the age
readings: 0.148). Starting values of the growth parameters: see
data-raw/whiting_example.R.
